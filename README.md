# E-commerce Sales Performance Dashboard using Microsoft Excel

## Objective:
To design an interactive dashboard in Excel that provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance across multiple dimensions such as category, sub-category, state, segment, and year. The dashboard aims to identify key trends, profitable areas, and YoY growth for data-driven decision-making.

## Dataset Overview:
Source: Sample E-commerce dataset
Total Records: 9,994
Key Fields: Order Date, Sales, Profit, Quantity, Category, Sub-Category, State, Segment, Region, Year

## Tools & Techniques Used:
* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
* Slicers and Timeline Filters
* Conditional Formatting
* Data Validation & Drop-downs
* Line, Bar, Pie Charts
* Custom Calculations (Profit Margin, YoY Growth)

## Key KPIs Analyzed:
* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Quantity Sold
* Number of Orders
* Profit Margin (%)
* YoY Growth (%)

## Dashboard Features:
* KPI Summary Cards: Sales, Profit, Quantity, Orders, and Profit Margin with YoY growth.
* Sales and Profit Trends: Monthly bar chart to track changes over time.
* Category Analysis: Category-wise profit and sales contribution, Pie chart for sales % distribution
* Geographic Analysis: Sales by State using a horizontal bar chart
* Product Analysis: Top 5 Sub-Categories by Sales
* Filters: Interactive slicers for Year, Segment, and Region

## Insights & Recommendations:
* Technology emerged as the most profitable category.
* Chairs and Phones were among the top-selling sub-categories.
* States like California and New York showed highest sales volume.
* Profit margins improved YoY by 13.6%, indicating operational efficiency.
* Recommends focusing promotional efforts on high-growth segments and top-performing categories.

## Outcome:
* Delivered an intuitive, interactive dashboard enabling real-time decision-making.
* Simplified complex data into actionable insights for business strategy and performance optimization.
